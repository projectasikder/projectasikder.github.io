About

From sociology to statistics — I study how people live, then use data to show it clearly.

I started in sociology, researching child marriage and cyberbullying in rural and urban Bangladesh. That work pulled me toward the numbers behind the stories — which is why I’m now finishing a Master’s in Applied Statistics and Data Science.

Today I split my time between the field and the dataset: running focus group discussions and in-depth interviews for reproductive health research at BRAC JPGSPH, then cleaning, modelling, and writing up what the data actually says — in Stata, Python, and plain language.
